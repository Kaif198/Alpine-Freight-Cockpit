    DSV expands Air ThermoDirect network with direct Shanghai connections to global pharma hubs

    DSV is expanding its global Air ThermoDirect network with a new direct connection between Luxembourg and Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG), with additional US routes planned. The expansion further strengthens DSV's temperature-controlled air freight capabilities and supports growing demand for reliable pharmaceutical logistics.
